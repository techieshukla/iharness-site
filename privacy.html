Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-21

What iHarness is

iHarness is a self-hosted, local-first gateway and client for running AI coding agents on your own machines. Your code, credentials, conversations, and subscriptions stay on devices you control.

Information we collect

The gateway runs on hardware you own. It does not send your source code, conversation transcripts, or project data to the operators of iHarness. Optional Google Sign-In is used only to verify your email address; we do not access your Google contacts, files, email, or calendar.

If you enable push notifications, your browser shares a push subscription token with the gateway you configure. That token is stored on the same gateway and is not shared with third parties.

How information is used

  • To authenticate you to your own gateway.
  • To deliver background push notifications about agent activity.
  • To keep the local workspace state in sync across your devices.

Third parties

Google Sign-In and browser push services are provided by Google and your browser vendor. Communication with AI backends (e.g. OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenCode) happens directly from your gateway using keys you supply.

Your choices

You can revoke browser notification permission, remove the PWA, or delete the gateway installation at any time. Google Sign-In can be disabled by removing the OAuth configuration from your gateway.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the operator of the iHarness instance you are using.
